MemoHoney™ Ingredients

MemoHoney™ is marketed as a daily cognitive-support formula. Current product information lists the following core ingredients, with some online versions showing differences in the complete label.

1

L-Arginine

L-Arginine is an amino acid involved in the body's nitric-oxide pathway. It is included to support healthy circulation and normal blood flow, including circulation that supports everyday brain function.
2

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

This form combines L-arginine with alpha-ketoglutarate. It is included in the formula to provide additional amino-acid and circulation support as part of MemoHoney's overall cognitive-wellness approach.
3

L-Citrulline HCl

L-Citrulline is an amino acid that the body can convert into L-arginine. It supports the body's natural nitric-oxide pathway and healthy vascular function.
4

L-Citrulline Malate

This combination of citrulline and malate is commonly used in nutritional formulas supporting circulation and cellular energy metabolism. In MemoHoney™, it complements the other amino-acid ingredients.
5

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

Niacin is an essential B vitamin that contributes to normal energy metabolism and nervous-system function. The currently listed label provides 7.5 mg NE per capsule (47% DV).
6

Beta-Alanine

Beta-alanine is an amino acid commonly used in nutritional formulas designed to support physical and cellular endurance. MemoHoney's listed formula contains 40 mg per capsule.
7

Calcium

Calcium, listed as calcium carbonate, contributes to normal nerve transmission and other essential physiological functions. The current label lists 18 mg per capsule (1.5% DV).
